## What these boards ask of you

These five add no new mechanic. The pressure is the same three the Expert band has always applied: sand keeps coming, one mis-aimed pull can cut off a pit you still need, and the clock runs the whole time you are deciding. What separates a clean clear from a restart is almost always the order the colors go out in.

- **Read the whole board before the first pull.** Count how much of each color is on the grid and find the pit that wants the most of it. That survey is what makes the right order visible, and it costs seconds rather than a restart.
- **Take the heaviest pit while you still have room.** A big pit needs the longest uninterrupted flow, so opening it early while the board is still open beats feeding it late through a narrow gap.
- **Order beats speed.** There is no move limit, only the clock. Choosing the next color deliberately is faster than correcting a wrong one.
- **Restart instead of rescuing.** Once a board turns into a tangle, a replay is nearly always cheaper than untangling it — watch the order in the video and start clean.
