## What these boards ask of you

These five introduce no new mechanic. The Expert band applies the same three pressures it always has: sand keeps arriving, one misplaced pull can cut off a pit you still need, and the clock runs while you decide. What usually separates a clean clear from a restart is the order the colors leave the board.

- **Survey the whole board first.** Count how much of each color is available and locate the pit that needs the largest share of it. That survey is what makes the right order visible, and it costs seconds instead of a restart.
- **Open the heaviest pit while there is room.** A large pit needs the longest uninterrupted flow, so starting it early while the board is open beats feeding it late through a narrow gap.
- **Order beats speed.** There is no move limit, only a timer. Choosing the next color deliberately is faster than correcting a wrong pull.
- **Restart instead of rescuing.** Once a board becomes a tangle, a clean replay is usually cheaper than untangling it — watch the order in the video and start again.
