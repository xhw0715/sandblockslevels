## What these boards ask of you

Nothing in the 560s introduces a new mechanic — the pressure is the same three things the Expert band has always applied: sand keeps arriving, one mis-aimed pull can cut off a pit you still need, and the timer keeps running while you decide. What separates a clean clear from a restart is almost always the order the colors go out in.

- **Read the whole board before the first pull.** Count how much of each color is sitting on the grid and find the pit that wants the most of it. That survey is what makes the right order obvious, and it costs a few seconds instead of a restart.
- **Take the heaviest pit while you still have room.** A big pit needs the longest uninterrupted flow, so opening it early while the board is still open beats trying to feed it late through a narrow gap.
- **Order beats speed.** There is no move limit, only the clock. Deciding the next color deliberately is faster than correcting a wrong one.
- **Restart instead of rescuing.** Once a board turns into a tangle, the replay is nearly always cheaper than untangling it — watch the video's order and start clean.
