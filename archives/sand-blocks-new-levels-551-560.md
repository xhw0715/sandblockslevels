## What these Expert boards ask of you

Past level 550 the boards stop being generous. Ten levels in one batch is a real jump in the Expert band, and they share the same three pressures: sand keeps arriving, one wrong pull can wall off a pit you still need, and the timer does not pause while you think. Four habits carry most of them:

- **Scan before you pull.** Find every color on the board and the pit holding the most grains of it before committing to anything. The survey is what makes the order obvious.
- **Open the biggest pit first.** The largest flow takes the longest to drain, so filling it early frees the most space for every pull after it.
- **Plan the order, not the speed.** There is no move limit here, only the clock. A few seconds spent choosing the next color is cheaper than a restart.
- **Reset rather than salvage.** If a board turns into a tangle mid-run, restart and replay the order from the video. A fresh board is always cheaper than untangling sand.
